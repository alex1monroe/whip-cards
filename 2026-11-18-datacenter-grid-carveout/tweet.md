# BREAKING: Data-center grid-cost carve-out in Senate permitting bill (draft, not posted)

Quote: Bloomberg X 2123251200082452804

BREAKING: Authors of the Senate's energy permitting bill are proposing an exemption that would let some data centers skip its requirement to pay for power-grid upgrades, Bloomberg reports.

The carve-out covers sites that mainly provide internet, cable or telecom services. A final Senate vote is expected this week, per Reuters.
