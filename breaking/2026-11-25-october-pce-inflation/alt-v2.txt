Whip illustration. BREAKING, cost of living: Inflation gauge rises to 3.8%. Prices rose 3.8% in October from a year earlier, up from 3.7% in September, the Commerce Department said. Excluding food and energy: 3.3%, up from 3.1% and 0.1 point above the forecast. Month over month: up 0.3%, down from a 0.5% rise in September, even as gas prices cooled, per AP. Q3 economy: 4.3% annualized growth, revised up from 3.4%. Prices are still rising at nearly twice the Fed's 2% target. Inflation-adjusted consumer spending was flat in October. CNBC reports the data likely seal a Fed rate hike at its next meeting. Sources: BEA; AP; CNBC; CNN and Axios.
