# Sources: Air Force Cloud One breach (Nov 27, 2026)

Draft only. Not posted or scheduled.

- Reuters exclusive, Mike Stone and Raphael Satter, Nov 27, 2026 (~6 AM ET): "Hackers breach US Air Force Cloud One computing platform"
  - Mirror: https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/hackers-breach-us-air-force-cloud-one-computing-platform-6587501
  - Reuters X post: https://x.com/Reuters/status/2126279085215391931

## Facts used on the card / tweet
- Hackers breached Cloud One, the Air Force's flagship cloud computing platform, per three people familiar who spoke to Reuters; discovered within roughly the past month during upgrade work.
- Who did it, full scope, and whether data was stolen: unknown (Reuters could not establish).
- Cloud One (started 2017) combines Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle and Alphabet; runs apps for personnel data and pay, logistics and maintenance; includes Cloud-Based Command and Control (North American air-defense radar feeds) and the Theater Integrated Combat Munitions System (tracks bombs and missiles at 200+ USAF locations worldwide).
- Air Force statement: it "does not comment on the security posture, vulnerabilities or operational status of specific networks and systems."

## Context (not on the card)
- One source: intruders exploited a software vulnerability to reach the control plane; Cyber Command called in to assess damage (single source, kept off the card).
- Back-office migrations paused; updates blocked; some admins lost rights.
- Leidos (prime contractor since 2025) referred questions to the Air Force.
- Reuters: the US government is dealing with at least six other recently disclosed cyber incidents (two at FBI, Pentagon data, DHS personnel leak, two spy agencies, Treasury).
- No motives, no attribution of blame.
