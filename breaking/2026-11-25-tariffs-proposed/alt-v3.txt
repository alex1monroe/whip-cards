Whip illustration. UPDATE, tariffs and trade: New tariffs proposed; China faces an added 20%. The U.S. trade representative proposed tariffs on 14 trading partners after an eight-month Section 301 probe into excess capacity, Reuters reports. China: an added 20% on top of first-term tariffs, within the cap of a trade truce that expires Jan. 10, 2027. EU, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan: within the 15% caps in existing deals. India, Vietnam and seven more: rates vary; partners without finalized deals remain in talks; Malaysia within its 19% cap. Mexico and Norway still under review. A comment period and hearing come first, which could push the tariffs into January 2027. Rates to be published in the Federal Register. The administration says these partners create structural excess capacity. The pushback: China rejected the excess-capacity charge in a July paper. A U.S. official said Washington doesn't expect China to retaliate. Sources: AP; Reuters; BBC; MOFCOM; Federal Register.
