Whip illustration. Update, government ethics: Schumer: Huang broke export law. Two columns. The accusation: Schumer, Nov. 24, said Huang "shipped extremely advanced chips to China, violating US export control law," comparing it to "selling rocket engines to the Soviets during the Space Race," and urged bipartisan export-control bills. The responses: Vance, Nov. 25, said the accusations "were based on anonymous reporting, not on actual evidence" and that he was told Huang is under no investigation. AG Blanche concluded there was no evidence Huang broke any laws, Reuters reports. Nvidia said its shipments were legal and government-authorized. Timeline: Nov. 23, board reinstates Huang; Nov. 24, Schumer's post; Nov. 25, Vance; Dec. 1-3, Huang due before House panels and Senate Banking. Also: Megaspeed is under criminal investigation, the NYT reported; GOP Sen. Cotton backs chip export bills.
