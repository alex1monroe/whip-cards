Whip illustration. UPDATE, Hadrian / 1789 Capital: Raskin widens 1789 Capital probe, AP reports. Timeline: July 2025, 1789 Capital invests in Hadrian, an AI-and-robotics defense manufacturer. Aug. 2026, Rep. Jamie Raskin, the ranking Democrat on House Judiciary, opens a probe of 1789 Capital. Nov. 23, Raskin sends letters to Hadrian, Juul, Polymarket and GrabAGun seeking records tied to 1789 Capital, due Dec. 7. Nov. 24, ProPublica reports the Pentagon is weighing a $15 billion loan to Hadrian. The Pentagon disputes the figure. The letters seek communications with 1789 Capital, plus investment agreements, valuations and due-diligence files. Attorney Bill Burck told Fox Business, "These are personal attacks untethered to actual evidence." Raskin is examining whether the firm helped steer federal contracts or regulatory decisions, or used advance knowledge of them, House Judiciary Democrats said. Sources: AP; House Judiciary Democrats letters, Nov. 23; Fox Business; ProPublica, Nov. 24.
