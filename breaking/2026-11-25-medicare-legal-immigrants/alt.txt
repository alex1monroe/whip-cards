Whip illustration. BREAKING, health care: Medicare cutoff for legal immigrants. Social Security letters tell an estimated 300,000 legal immigrants their Medicare will end, The Washington Post reported. Timeline: July 2025, the tax and spending law (H.R. 1) limits Medicare to citizens, green-card holders, Cuban-Haitian entrants and COFA migrants. Nov. 2026, Social Security starts mailing letters. Dec. 2026, CMS mails a follow-up letter on pink paper. Feb. 1, 2027, coverage ends for those who can't confirm an eligible status. Who is affected: asylum seekers, refugees and temporary visa holders, per CMS. CMS says the changes affect "a limited number of people with Medicare." Advocates warn dialysis patients could lose coverage. Sources: The Washington Post; CMS; The Herald Business.
