# Thread outline: Puerto Rico power-bill protest
Suggested slot: **Sat Nov 28, 2026, ~9:00 AM ET** (within 12 hours of the evening BREAKING post). Draft only; route through Approver, then wait for Alex's "post". Nothing scheduled.

1. **Hook (card.png):** Puerto Rico homes paid 25.15¢ per kWh in September, vs. 18.17¢ U.S. average (EIA Form 861M, preliminary). On Black Friday, thousands marched in San Juan. 🧵
2. **What happened:** About 30 groups (unions, retirees, students, bus drivers, religious groups) rallied at Plaza Las Américas for a "Day of National Outrage" over electric rates, cost of living and basic services. (AP) EFE counted 4,000+ and reported the Expreso Las Américas was blocked. (EFE)
3. **The bills:** AP: monthly bills for some residential customers recently rose about 18%, from $229 to $271. EFE: power rates rose 18.5% last quarter. (AP; EFE)
4. **Reliability:** One retiree told AP his neighborhood loses power at least once a week; a student said hers went out 12 times this week. (AP)
5. **The LUMA ruling:** On Nov 25, Puerto Rico's Supreme Court voted 5-4 to void LUMA Energy's contract extension and ordered an orderly transition within one year, supervised by the Court of First Instance. (Tribunal Supremo release)
6. **Other side:** Gov. Jenniffer González praised the ruling (AP). The Association of Industries warned cancellation could "discourage private investment" (AP); the Oversight Board said the grid should be run by a private operator, not a public utility (EFE).
7. **What's next:** Organizers pledged to keep pushing; demands include higher wages and pensions. EFE: of ~143,000 public pensioners, 55,000+ get under $1,000/month. Who runs the grid after LUMA, and what happens to rates? (AP; EFE)
