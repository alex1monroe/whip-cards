Whip illustration. NEW, government ethics: Lutnick's detail drew on chip agents. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick reassigned export-enforcement agents to his personal security detail, The Atlantic reports. The agents are from the Bureau of Industry and Security, whose work includes stopping illegal exports of AI chips; BIS has fewer than 200 special agents. They traveled on his private jet and guarded his residences, according to five people familiar. Typically three or four were on Lutnick at a time, two former officials said. In April, Lutnick asked Congress for 290 more enforcement officers to fight chip smuggling. Commerce says the assignments were temporary and meant to add protection amid credible threats. Publicly reported export enforcement actions roughly halved compared with 2023 and 2024, per data The Atlantic cited. Sources: The Atlantic; Commerce Department.
