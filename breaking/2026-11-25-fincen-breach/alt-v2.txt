Whip illustration. Headline: Treasury financial-crimes bureau breached. FinCEN, Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, says there was unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems; it took some systems offline; BSA E-Filing remains operational. A grid titled "Federal systems hit, recently reported" shows four shield tiles. FinCEN is highlighted in red as new, disclosed Nov. 24. The others: a CIA/ODNI unclassified intel-analysis system, resolved with classified systems unaffected, a person familiar said; the FBI's National Crime Information Center; and Agriculture Dept. applications taken offline, disrupting tools that support farm payments. Officials have not publicly linked these to FinCEN's incident. What this means: banks' filing deadlines are extended to Dec. 30; FinCEN hasn't said what data was accessed or who was responsible; officials have not publicly tied it to the other intrusions. Sources: FinCEN notice, ABC News, Nextgov/FCW, The Verge. Nov. 25, 2026.
