Whip illustration. Headline: Treasury crime unit breached. FinCEN, Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, says there was unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems; it took some systems offline, and the BSA E-Filing site remains operational. A grid titled "Federal systems hit, recently reported" shows four shield tiles. FinCEN is highlighted in red as new, disclosed Nov. 24. The others: a CIA/ODNI unclassified intelligence-analysis system, resolved with classified systems unaffected, a person familiar said; the FBI's National Crime Information Center; and Agriculture Department applications taken offline, disrupting tools that support payments to farmers. Officials have not linked these incidents. What this means: banks' filing deadlines are extended to Dec. 30; FinCEN hasn't said what data was accessed or who was responsible; it isn't tied to the other intrusions. Sources: FinCEN notice, ABC News, Nextgov/FCW, The Verge. Nov. 25, 2026.
