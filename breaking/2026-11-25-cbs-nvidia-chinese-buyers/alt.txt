Whip illustration. UPDATE, National Security News: Nvidia's California buyers with Chinese owners. A CBS News investigation found two California companies with priority access to Nvidia's most advanced chips are owned by Chinese firms. First chain: Nvidia sells to RiCloud AI of San Jose, an Nvidia "preferred" cloud partner, whose parent is Yuxing InfoTech of Shenzhen, China. Nvidia's filings count these as U.S. sales. The U.S. government has not accused RiCloud or Yuxing of wrongdoing. Second chain: Nvidia lists Aivres, a California server maker formerly named Inspur, as an "elite" partner. Its Chinese parent Inspur's group firms were blacklisted in March 2023. Aivres itself is not blacklisted and did not respond to CBS. Nvidia has not said what background checks it ran on RiCloud. Sen. Chris Coons is poised to investigate. Sources: CBS News; New York Times analysis cited by CBS.
