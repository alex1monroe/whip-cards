Whip illustration. Breaking, free speech: Judge blocks FCC's early review of ABC licenses. A timeline runs from April 27, Trump: Kimmel "should be immediately fired by Disney and ABC," through a giant yellow 1 DAY to April 28, FCC orders early review of ABC licenses. Below: Judge: ABC likely to prove the review was retaliation. FCC: Its review grew out of a diversity probe. Full timeline: April 27, after a Jimmy Kimmel skit about first lady Melania Trump, Trump posts on Truth Social. April 28, the FCC orders early license renewals for the 8 stations ABC owns, citing its probe of Disney's diversity practices; none were due before 2028. August 18, ABC, Disney and the stations sue. October 6, at a hearing, the FCC argues the case belongs in the appeals court. November 25, Judge Loren AliKhan bars further FCC action on the renewals while the case goes on; "This is not a close case," she wrote. The order is preliminary. Source: ABC v. FCC, D.D.C., Nov. 25 opinion.
