Below: Judge Loren AliKhan, U.S. District Court. FCC: The ruling is "wrong on the facts and wrong on the law." A stylized broadcast tower sits at lower right. Timeline: April 27, after a Jimmy Kimmel skit about first lady Melania Trump, Trump posts on Truth Social that Kimmel "should be immediately fired by Disney and ABC." April 28, the FCC orders early license renewals for the 8 stations ABC owns, citing its probe of Disney's diversity practices; none were due before 2028. August 18, ABC, Disney and the stations sue. October 6, at a hearing, the FCC argues the case belongs in the appeals court. November 25, the judge finds ABC likely to prove the review was retaliation and bars further FCC action on the renewals while the case goes on. The order is preliminary. Source: ABC v. FCC, D.D.C., Nov. 25 opinion.
