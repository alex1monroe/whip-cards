Whip illustration. Breaking, free speech: Judge blocks FCC review of ABC licenses. A federal judge found ABC likely to prove the FCC's early license review was retaliation for its speech; "This is not a close case," she wrote. Timeline: April 23, Jimmy Kimmel jokes about first lady Melania Trump in a skit on his ABC show. April 27, Trump posts on Truth Social that Kimmel should be fired by Disney and ABC. April 28, the next day, the FCC orders early license renewals for the 8 stations ABC owns, citing its probe of Disney's diversity practices; none were due before 2028. August 18, ABC, Disney and the stations sue. October 6, at a hearing, the FCC argues the case belongs in the appeals court. November 25, Judge Loren AliKhan bars further FCC action on the renewals while the case goes on. What this means: the order is preliminary; the FCC says the review grew out of its diversity probe; the judge said the FCC can still use other investigative tools.
