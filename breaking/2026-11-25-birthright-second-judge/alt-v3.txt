Whip illustration. Breaking, courts: 2nd judge blocks new birthright order. Judge Christopher Cooper in D.C. wrote "the President cannot carve out new exceptions to birthright citizenship." Supreme Court's 3 exceptions: children of diplomats with full immunity, of hostile occupying forces, and born on foreign public ships. Added by the order, now blocked, as Cooper described them: children whose parents work for foreign governments or international organizations but lack diplomatic immunity; children born through commercial surrogacy arrangements; children with a parent suspected of membership in certain gangs or terrorist organizations. Timeline 2026: June 30, Supreme Court strikes first order, 6-3. Aug. 6, EO 14418 signed. Sept. 2, Judge Boardman (Md.) blocks it. Nov. 25, Judge Cooper (D.C.) blocks it. In September, the White House said the order is consistent with the Supreme Court's ruling; it hasn't commented on today's ruling. Cooper denied a stay pending appeal.
