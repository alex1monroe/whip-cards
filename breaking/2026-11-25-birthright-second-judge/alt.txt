Whip illustration. Breaking, courts: 2nd judge blocks new birthright order. Judge Christopher Cooper in Washington, D.C., wrote "the President cannot carve out new exceptions to birthright citizenship." Comparison, who is not a citizen at birth. Supreme Court's 3 exceptions: children of diplomats with full immunity, of hostile occupying forces, and born on foreign public ships. Added by the order, now blocked: children of foreign-government or international-organization workers without diplomatic immunity; births tied to a "commercial transaction," including surrogacy and birth tourism; children of parents labeled "alien enemies." Timeline 2026: June 30, Supreme Court strikes first order, 6-3. Aug. 6, EO 14418 signed. Sept. 2, Judge Boardman (Md.) blocks it. Nov. 25, Judge Cooper (D.C.) blocks it. The White House says the order fits the Supreme Court ruling; Cooper denied a stay pending appeal.
