Whip illustration. Update, government ethics: S&P puts Nvidia on downgrade watch. The ratings agency says Jensen Huang's departure and reinstatement don't answer its questions about how Nvidia's board oversees its CEO. Left: S&P's top grades, AAA, AA+, AA and AA-minus. Nvidia is at AA, now on CreditWatch Negative; S&P expects any cut to be limited to one notch, to AA-minus. Right, what S&P flagged: board oversight of executive conduct; board independence, citing long director tenure; weak executive oversight and no succession plan, with CNBC reporting Huang told staff Tuesday Nvidia doesn't need one; and China exposure and internal controls. Next: S&P meets the board and management; Huang is due before House panels and Senate Banking Dec. 1 to 3; S&P decides within 90 days. Nvidia keeps its AA for now. On Nov. 17 its board created an export-control oversight committee. S&P's note doesn't mention the administration's role.
