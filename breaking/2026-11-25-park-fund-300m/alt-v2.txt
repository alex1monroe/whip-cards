Whip illustration. New, government ethics: Who gave $347 million? A National Park Service donation account took in $347 million in September, The New York Times reports. Bar chart: September projected $50 million vs. collected $347 million. Before Trump returned, the account took in less than $100 million a year; it has received about $790 million since, per the Times. Records don't name the source, or say if it was one gift or several, and the administration declined to say where it came from or what it will fund. Trump has used the account to help pay for the White House ballroom, Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool repairs and design work on a planned triumphal arch. Interior spokesperson Katie Martin said Trump has done more to make the capital a shining beacon than any other president. Tony Irish of PEER: "Are the donors seeking certain government benefits? We just don't know, because of the lack of transparency." Source: The New York Times, Nov. 24, via Mediaite and Quartz.
