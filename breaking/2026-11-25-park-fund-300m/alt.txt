Whip illustration. New, government ethics: Who gave $347 million? A National Park Service donation account took in $347 million in September 2026, The New York Times reports. Bar chart on one scale: September projected $50 million vs. actual $347 million; before Trump returned to office, less than $100 million a year, vs. about $790 million since. Records don't name the source or sources, or say if it was one gift or several, and the administration declined to say where it came from or what it will fund. Trump has used the account to help pay for the White House ballroom, Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool repairs and design work on a planned triumphal arch. Interior spokesperson Katie Martin said Trump has done more to make the capital a shining beacon than any other president. Tony Irish of PEER, a former Interior attorney: "We just don't know, because of the lack of transparency." Source: The New York Times, Nov. 24, 2026.
