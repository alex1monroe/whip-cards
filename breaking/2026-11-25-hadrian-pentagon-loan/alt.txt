Whip illustration. BREAKING, corruption & ethics: Pentagon weighs loan to Trump Jr.-linked startup. ProPublica reports a $15 billion loan is under discussion for Hadrian Automation; the Pentagon disputes the figure. Timeline: Nov. 2024, Donald Trump Jr. joins venture firm 1789 Capital as a partner. July 2025, 1789 Capital invests in Hadrian. Summer 2026, Hadrian raises $1.37 billion with "major participation by 1789 Capital." Nov. 24, ProPublica reports the Pentagon weighs a loan to build a dozen automated factories, almost twice the company's value. The Pentagon says talks are in early stages and political connections "play absolutely no role." 1789 Capital says it holds a minority stake and stays out of government contracts. Former defense officials told ProPublica the deal could run afoul of long-standing guidance on protecting taxpayer money. Sources: ProPublica; PR Newswire; Pentagon and 1789 Capital statements.
