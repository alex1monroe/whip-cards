Whip illustration. Headline: "Stock ban vote next week." House leaders teed up Rep. Bryan Steil's Stop Insider Trading Act (H.R. 11142) for a floor vote, Bloomberg Government reports. Two columns. What it does: bans lawmakers, their spouses and dependent children from buying stock in public companies; members could sell with 7 to 14 days' notice, per Punchbowl News. What it doesn't: require divesting current holdings, which Democrats have called for; cover the president, which some Democrats want. The road so far: the House earlier passed the ban in a bill that also required photo voter ID; that version failed a Senate procedural vote in late September; the House Rules Committee meets Nov. 30 at 4 p.m.; a floor vote is expected next week. What this means: it's split off from voter ID; Democrats want a wider ban and say they'll prioritize one when they take control next year. Sources: Bloomberg Government, House Rules Committee, Punchbowl News.
