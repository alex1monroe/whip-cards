Whip illustration. Headline: "Stock ban vote next week." House leaders teed up Rep. Bryan Steil's Stop Insider Trading Act (H.R. 11142) for a floor vote, Bloomberg Government reports. Two columns. What it does: bans lawmakers, their spouses and dependent children from buying stock in public companies; members could sell with 7–14 days' notice (H.R. 11142). What it doesn't: require divesting current holdings, which Democrats have called for; start immediately (180 days after enactment). Road so far: the House earlier passed the ban in a bill that also required photo voter ID; that version failed a late-September Senate vote; Rules meets Nov. 30 at 4 p.m.; a floor vote is expected next week. What this means: it's split off from voter ID; Democrats want a wider ban that requires divestment, and some also want it to cover the president; they say they'll prioritize a ban once they take control next year. Sources: Bloomberg Government, H.R. 11142, House Rules Committee.
