Whip illustration. Investigation, fentanyl: 1.4 million pills go unseized. They moved through hotels, apartment complexes, Airbnb rentals and self-storage facilities in six states, an AP investigation found. Timeline: August 2024, DEA agents stake out a hotel and watch a deal of as many as 100,000 pills go unseized. April 2025, a record bust of 4 million fentanyl pills, separate from the 1.4 million that went unseized. August 17, 2026, the Justice Department's inspector general opens a review of department-wide policies, using a judgmental sample across multiple jurisdictions. November 24, 2026, AP counts at least 1.4 million additional pills that went unseized between August 2024 and April 2025. The DEA previously said its decisions were lawful and coordinated with federal prosecutors to prevent public harm. Spokesperson Amanda Wozniak: “Our response has not changed.” Almost all of the monitored pills remain unaccounted for, AP reported. Sources: AP, Nov. 24; DOJ OIG, Aug. 17.
