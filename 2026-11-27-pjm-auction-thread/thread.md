# Thread draft: PJM won't delay the Dec. 9 power auction (Nov. 27, 2026)

Draft only. Not posted, scheduled, or texted.

Why: Whip voter-priority ranks (Nov. 26 snapshot, polls as of Nov. 23): 'Electricity bills & data centers' is the No. 2 issue in Cost of Living (No. 1 domain) and No. 1 in Environment, Energy & Utilities; hard deadline Dec. 9 and Pa. bills rise Dec. 1.

Card (post 1): card.png (2440x3092); Kick 16:9: card_wide.png (3840x2160). 40 words.

## 1/6 (265 chars)

The power auction behind electric bills for 67 million people opens Dec. 9, and it isn't moving.

PJM's board told consumer advocates from 7 states on Nov. 25 that a delay this late "would be disruptive to the markets as a whole." Only FERC can order one, PJM says.

## 2/6 (274 chars)

Advocates from Ohio, Pa., N.J., Md., Del., W.Va. and Ill. asked for the delay Nov. 18, warning of "billions of dollars in unnecessary capacity costs" from speculative data-center demand.

Ohio's Maureen Willis: "Data centers should be responsible for the costs they create."

## 3/6 (276 chars)

PJM's own independent market monitor also urged a delay. It says data-center load has already added $29.4 billion in capacity costs for all customers.

It says the December auction is effectively ensured to clear at the maximum price, adding another $5 billion to $10 billion.

## 4/6 (280 chars)

PJM's side: Board Chair Paula Conboy agreed data-center demand is "a root cause" of the supply crunch.

But she wrote the price cap matches the last three auctions, so capacity costs "would not rise from where they are now," and that states, not PJM, decide what data centers pay.

## 5/6 (259 chars)

The bills are already moving. Pennsylvania's PUC says 10 of its 11 regulated electric utilities raise residential supply prices Dec. 1, including PECO and PPL at +10.3% each. The PUC says those prices are based on wholesale market rates. PJM runs that market.

## 6/6 (276 chars)

What's next: only FERC can delay the Dec. 9 auction, PJM says. The advocates cite January principles signed by all 13 PJM-state governors calling for "real and verified" data-center forecasts.

Should projected data-center demand count in the auction that helps set your bill?
